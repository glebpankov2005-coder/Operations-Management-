# OFFICIAL PROJECT BRIEF — "Project OTM" (On The Move)

Source: `Project DHL on the move_P1 2627.pdf` (client/university-provided). Transcribed verbatim-in-substance.

## Company details
- **DHL Supply Chain**, Site **Bemmel** (Assa Abloy operation).
- **Start:** 02-09-2026 · **Completion:** 31-10-2026.

## Project description
DHL Supply Chain is the world's largest logistics service provider (transport, warehousing,
e-commerce, etc.). In **Tiel**, DHL ran warehouse operations for **4 different clients**. The
warehouse was **recently relocated to a new site** (Bemmel). The move happened under a lot of
time pressure, so the Operations Manager believes the **current warehouse design is not optimal**.
The challenge: develop your own warehouse design that is potentially more efficient and effective
than the current one. Design choices (layout, storage concept, zones, equipment) have a big
impact on performance. Task: develop a design **based on a thorough analysis of Assa Abloy data**,
and outline the expected performance of your design.

## Scope / conditions
- Targets **receiving, storage, picking, shipping**.
- **End-to-end** view: choices across the 4 processes must be aligned.
- **Facility design is out of scope.**
- **Legal aspects** (fire safety, compliance) should be considered.

## Company input
- Data & information about **SKUs, volume and inventory** from Assa Abloy.
- New site size/limits: **footprint max 7,000 m²**, **height 12.2 m**.

## Deliverables
| # | Deliverable | Due |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Analysis** of data, pre-selection & justification of policies | 16-09-2026 |
| 2 | **Proposed future state** for the in-scope processes | 07-10-2026 |
| 3 | **Performance overview** incl. financial analysis | 21-10-2026 |

### Deliverable 1 — Analysis (details)
Determine design requirements from the data:
- SKUs and **pallets to be stored**
- **Orders** to be processed
- **Volume** to be stored / processed (total & per SKU)
- **Delivery lead time** (time between order received and sent)
- **Pallet weights / dimensions**
- **Picking requirements** (full pallets, cases, eaches)
- **Order profile** (lines per order, units per line, lines per pick, etc.)
- **Peak activity** levels — and more
Then: identify & select **design choices** (racking, setup, automation, …); define & substantiate
**policies** (batching, zoning, routing, putaway, slotting, sorting, …); identify & select
**equipment** (MHE, scanners, …); and **align all** of the above.

### Deliverable 2 — Future state (details)
- Define **process steps** (what fulfils the customer agreement; how to work in the system per step 1).
- **Flowcharts** showing where/how the step-1 design choices are implemented.
- Propose a **3-dimensional layout & zoning** that proves it meets the design requirements.
- Propose a **capacity plan** (expected productivity; quantify required labour & equipment).

### Deliverable 3 — Performance overview (details)
- Identify suitable **KPIs**; show expected performance per KPI for the design.
- Convert the capacity plan into expected **operating costs**: list **CAPEX & OPEX**.
- Finalise supporting documents (calculations, report) justifying step 1 & 2 choices, with sources.
